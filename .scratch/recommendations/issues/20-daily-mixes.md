# 20: Daily Mixes

**What to build:** Several Daily Mixes, each grouping the owner's listening into one style (clustered by sound fingerprints and the artist graph). Each is mostly library favourites plus some new tracks in the same style. They refresh daily at local midnight and are named by their main artists.

**Blocked by:** 14, 19

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Clustering produces a stable number of Mixes (e.g. up to six) that doesn't reshuffle wildly day to day
- [ ] Each Mix follows the Adventurousness balance (mostly known by default)
- [ ] Mixes are named after their top artists ("Daft Punk, Justice and more")
- [ ] Deterministic for a given day's seed and inputs (ADR 0001)
